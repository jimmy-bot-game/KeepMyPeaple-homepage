Keep My People

A private map for the people who agreed to be on it.

Stay close. Stay safe. Keep My People lets a group share live location, chat, and an SOS alert. Sharing starts only after you turn it on, and the people who can see you are the members of a group you joined.

How a group works

Everyone in the group chooses.

Location moves only between members of the same group, and only while that person has sharing turned on.

You join

Groups are invite-only.

Create a group or enter with a code, link, or QR code someone sent you. A group holds up to 20 members.

You share

The switch starts off.

Live location goes out only after you turn sharing on and allow location permission. You can turn it off at any time.

You leave

Leaving closes that room.

When you leave a group, you stop sharing into it and stop seeing that group’s map and chat.

What is in the app

The features that ship today.

Each item below is part of Keep My People on Android. Premium items are marked in the plans table.

01

Live group map

Open a group and see members who are sharing. Your own pin follows the phone’s GPS. Other members appear from updates the app receives while they share. Profiles can show battery level, whether sharing is on, and when a person was last seen.

  • While the app is open, movement updates are finer.
  • In the background, updates are slower so the battery lasts longer. Android can still pause an app.
  • If the phone is offline, the latest point is sent when the connection returns. The offline trail is not replayed as a live path.
02

Several groups, one account

You can belong to more than one group. Each group has its own members, map, chat, and SOS. Switch the active group inside the app. Invites can be copied, shared, or shown as a QR code, and they expire.

03

Group chat and a meetup pin

Send messages to the people in the current group. You can also drop a location into chat, or pick a meetup spot — a saved place or your current location — and post it with an optional note so everyone heads to the same point. A typing indicator shows when someone is writing.

04

Places and nearby alerts

Save a place such as home, work, or school. The free plan stores 1 place. Premium stores up to 10. Place alerts tell you when a sharing member arrives or leaves. Distance alerts tell you when a member comes inside a radius you set, or leaves it. Both are available on the free plan.

05

SOS to the group

SOS notifies the other members of the current group and includes the last known location when the phone has one. It does not call police, medical services, or any public emergency number, and it does not record microphone audio.

  • Android: with the app allowed to run in the background, turn the screen on and off 5 or more times quickly with the side button. This can work with the screen already off. There is also an in-app SOS button with a 10-second cancel countdown.
  • iOS, once that app is released: the side button cannot be watched by an app. The design uses a firm shake of 3 or more times while Keep My People is open, or the in-app button.
  • People who receive SOS get vibration for about 15 seconds, including when the phone is on silent, because the alert uses the alarm stream. Premium can add sound. It stops if you leave the SOS screen, open the notification, or the sender ends the alert.
06

History, stays, and drive notes

Location history is kept for about 30 days, and only for times when sharing was on. Premium can play a route for today, yesterday, 7 days, or 30 days. You can open your own stay map on the free plan. Opening another member’s stay map is a Premium feature. Drive reports — trips, distance, time on the road, top speed, speeding, and hard acceleration — are included with Premium. A free account can preview a recent journey after a short ad. Premium skips that ad.

07

Premium safety alerts

Driving start and stop, speeding above a limit you choose, low battery, and inactivity (the phone has not been used for a stretch you set) are Premium alerts. Emergency SOS alerts themselves are available without Premium.

08

Profile, language, and diamonds

Sign in with email or Google. Set a display name and photo. The interface can follow the phone language or be set to English, Japanese, or Korean. Diamonds buy map decorations — frames, icons, hats, and crowns. You can watch an optional rewarded ad for a small diamond amount, or buy a diamond pack in Google Play. Decorations do not change who can see your location.

Limits worth knowing

A group alert, not a rescue service.

Keep My People tells the people in your group where a sharing phone is, within the accuracy of GPS, Wi-Fi, and the phone’s own power limits. A pin can be late, coarse, or missing indoors and underground. If someone is in danger, contact local emergency services directly.

Step-by-step setup, permissions, and SOS

What SOS sends

A push to the current group, the time, and location if the phone had one. Members can open that point on the group map. The sender can end the alert. There is no surrounding-audio upload.

Plans

Free, and one Premium subscription.

Premium is a monthly or yearly subscription through Google Play. The store shows the price for your country. If a price has not loaded yet, the app shows $4.99 a month and $39.99 a year as placeholders.
