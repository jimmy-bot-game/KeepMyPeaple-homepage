Delete your account

You can delete a Keep My People account from the Android app, or by email if you cannot open the app. There is no separate website login. The iOS app is in development, so use the Android app or the email steps below.

Uninstalling is not deletion.

Removing Keep My People from your phone leaves the account, location history, and chat on our servers. Follow one of the two paths below. After you request deletion you have 7 days to change your mind by signing in again. When that window ends, the account and personal data are permanently removed or irreversibly anonymized, except the records listed under “What we keep”.

Path 1 — In the Android app

This is the direct path. It starts the 7-day window immediately.

  1. Open Keep My People and sign in.
  2. Open the menu, then Settings.
  3. Choose Delete account.
  4. Read the notice. Type DELETE and confirm.
  5. The app signs you out and shows that deletion was requested.
  6. To cancel during the 7 days, sign in again with the same account and confirm that you want to keep it. The pending-deletion screen is where that recovery happens.
  7. If you do nothing, the account is permanently deleted after the 7 days.

Path 2 — Email, if you cannot open the app

Send the request from the email address on the account to help@trade-arena.net. Use the subject Account deletion request — Keep My People. Include the account email, and a display name if you have one. The same 7-day recovery window applies once we match the account: signing in during those 7 days cancels the request. If you no longer have that inbox, say so in the message and add anything that helps us find the account, such as the display name. We may ask a follow-up question before we schedule deletion.

There is no web form that deletes the account by itself. The form below only prepares that email and opens your mail app.

If your mail app did not open, copy the message below and send it to help@trade-arena.net.


        
      

    

    

    
What is deleted or anonymized

    
After the 7-day window, we delete or irreversibly anonymize personal data tied to the account, including:

    
    
      
  • The login, email identity on the account, display name, profile photo, optional phone number, and language preference.
    • 
      
  • Membership in groups. You are removed from those groups. The groups themselves remain for the other members.
    • 
      
  • Chat messages that are attributable to you, which are deleted or irreversibly anonymized. Messages written by other people stay in the group.
    • 
      
  • Live location, location history, saved places, and SOS events you sent.
    • 
      
  • Push notification tokens, alert preferences, diamond balance, and cosmetic items on the profile.
    • 
      
  • Support tickets, where we can remove them without keeping a record the law requires.
    • 
    

    
During the 7 days the data stays in place so you can recover the account by signing in.


    
What we keep, and for how long

    
    
      
  • Purchase and subscription records needed to honor Google Play refunds, chargebacks, fraud checks, and accounting duties. These are kept for the period the store dispute process or applicable law requires, then deleted or reduced to what that duty still needs. They are not used to rebuild a profile or to keep sharing your location.
    • 
      
  • A short security log of the deletion request itself, without your location history.
    • 
      
  • Information we are legally required to retain for a specific request. We do not keep it longer than that duty lasts.
    • 
      
  • Other members’ accounts, their locations, and their messages.
    • 
    


    

      Premium and diamonds are separate from account deletion.
      
Deleting the account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel Premium in the Play Store subscription page, or you may still be billed. Diamond packs already consumed are not refunded by account deletion. Refunds follow Google Play’s rules.
