Guide

How to use Keep My People

This guide matches the Android app. The iOS app is in development and is not on the App Store. Where iPhone behavior is described, it is the behavior built for the release, not something you can install today.

Create an account

  1. Install Keep My People from Google Play. The Android package name is com.keepmypeople.
  2. Create an account with email, or continue with Google Sign-In.
  3. If you use email, confirm the address when the app asks you to. Password reset also returns through the app link.
  4. Add a display name. A profile photo is optional. You can change both later from the menu, under profile.
  5. Choose a language in Settings if you do not want the phone’s language. English, Japanese, and Korean are available in the interface.

The app does not read your address book and does not ask you to connect a social-network friend list.

Open or join a group

  1. From the empty state, create a group and give it a name, or join with an invite code.
  2. To invite someone, open the invite screen for the current group. You can copy the code, share the link, or let the other person scan the QR code from inside their app.
  3. Invites expire, and a group stops accepting members at 20 people. If a join fails, ask for a fresh invite or check that the group is not full.
  4. You can belong to more than one group. The map, chat, places, and SOS you see belong to the group that is currently open. Switch groups from group management.
  5. To leave, open group management and leave that group. You stop sharing into it and stop seeing its map and chat. Other groups on the same account stay as they are.

Turn location sharing on

  1. Sharing stays off until you enable it. Open privacy controls in the menu and turn on “Share my location”, or use the sharing control on your own profile.
  2. When Android asks, allow location. For a live pin while you are in the app, “While using the app” is enough.
  3. If people should still see you after you leave the app, allow location all the time and leave sharing on. Also allow the app to keep running in the background, and exempt it from aggressive battery restrictions if your phone offers that setting. The in-app background screen explains the current permission state.
  4. Your pin on the map is drawn from the phone’s own GPS. Other people’s pins update when their phones successfully send a point. Expect a faster refresh with the app open, and a slower refresh in the background.
  5. Turn sharing off when you want the live updates to stop. History, when it is available, includes only periods when sharing was on.

GPS can be weak indoors, underground, or among tall buildings. A shown accuracy of tens of meters is normal. Keep My People does not promise a street-level fix in every place.

Read the map and a member profile

  1. Open the map for the current group. Members who are sharing appear as pins.
  2. Open a person for their profile: display name, photo, sharing state, battery percentage when the phone has reported one, and last seen time.
  3. Someone marked as not sharing is in the group, but their live location is not being published.
  4. If a pin looks old, that person may be offline, in a low-power state, or in a place with poor GPS. The app keeps the latest point it received. It does not invent a path to fill the gap.

Chat and set a meetup

  1. Open chat for the current group. Type a message and send. If sending fails, tap to retry.
  2. You can share a location into the conversation and open it again on the group map, or in Google Maps.
  3. For a meetup, choose a saved place or your current location, add an optional note, and share it. The note and a map link are posted into the group chat.
  4. Chat is visible to members of that group. Leaving the group removes your access to that room.

Save a place and choose alerts

  1. Open saved places from the menu. Add a name and an icon such as home, work, school, or a pin. You can save the phone’s current location.
  2. The free plan keeps 1 place. Premium keeps up to 10. Delete a place you no longer want.
  3. Open notification management. Place alerts and distance alerts can be switched on without Premium. Set the distance radius yourself.
  4. Driving start and stop, speeding, low battery, and inactivity alerts ask for Premium. Speeding uses a speed you set. Battery uses a percent threshold. Inactivity uses a duration you set.
  5. Emergency alerts control how an incoming SOS is presented. Free accounts get vibration. Premium can choose sound, vibration, or both.
  6. Push notifications also depend on the phone’s notification permission. If alerts never arrive, check that Keep My People is allowed to notify, and that battery saving has not stopped the app.

Send or receive SOS

SOS is a message to the current group. It is not a call to emergency services. If you need police, fire, or medical help, use the local emergency number on the phone.

  1. In-app button: start SOS, then wait through the 10-second countdown. Tap cancel if you started it by mistake. When the countdown finishes, members are notified with your location if the phone has one.
  2. Android side button: allow background running. Turn the screen on and off 5 or more times quickly. This is designed to work even if the screen was already off or the phone is in battery saver, as long as the app is still allowed to run.
  3. iOS, after release: apps cannot watch the side button. With Keep My People open, shake the phone firmly 3 or more times, or use the in-app button.
  4. If you receive an SOS, the phone vibrates for about 15 seconds on the alarm stream, so silent mode does not mute it. Premium can play a sound as well. Open the alert to see who sent it, the time, the accuracy when known, and a button to view the point on the group map. The alert ends if you leave that screen, tap the push, or the sender ends it.
  5. If location was unavailable, the alert still goes out and says that a location was not attached.

Look back at movement

  1. History is stored for about 30 days and only while sharing was on.
  2. Premium opens route playback for today, yesterday, 7 days, or 30 days, and opens drive reports for today or the current week: trip count, distance, time moving, top speed, speeding, and hard acceleration.
  3. You can view your own stay areas without Premium. Viewing another member’s stay map requires Premium.
  4. A free account can preview a recent journey after watching a short ad. Premium does not show that ad.

Subscribe, decorate, or write to support

  1. Open Premium from the menu. Choose monthly or yearly. Google Play completes the charge. The app unlocks Premium only after the purchase is verified. A pending purchase does not unlock the plan.
  2. A Play subscription stays linked to the Keep My People account that redeemed it. Signing into a second Keep My People account with the same Play account does not grant a second Premium.
  3. To cancel or resume, use the subscription page in Google Play. The app links you there. Premium remains until the paid period ends.
  4. The diamond shop sells cosmetic frames, icons, hats, and crowns for the map profile. Optional rewarded ads can add a small diamond amount after the ad finishes. Diamond packs are separate Play purchases.
  5. For help, use the in-app inquiry form, or email help@trade-arena.net. You can attach a screenshot in the app.

Delete the account when you want to leave

Uninstalling the app leaves the account in place. Use Settings → Delete account inside the app, or the account deletion page if you cannot open the app. You have 7 days to sign in and cancel the request. After that, personal data is removed or irreversibly anonymized, except records that must be kept.

Short answers

Who can see my location?

Only members of a group you joined, and only while your sharing switch is on. You can stop at any time.

Why is someone missing from the map?

They may have sharing off, they may have denied location permission, the phone may be offline or restricted by battery settings, or GPS may not have a fix. A full group will also refuse a new member past 20 people.

Does Premium remove ads?

Yes. Premium removes ads on the map and profile, and it unlocks history playback, drive reports, the extra alerts listed above, SOS sound, and up to 10 saved places.
