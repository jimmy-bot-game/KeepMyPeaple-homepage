Keep My People

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Keep My People (“we”, “us”, or the “Service”) explains here how information is collected, used, stored, shared, and deleted when you use the Keep My People mobile app and this website. The Service is built for mutual, opt-in location sharing inside groups you join. Live location is shown to members of those groups while sharing is on. We do not sell location data.

Location collection is also described in the Location Services Terms. The contract for using the Service is the Terms of Use. Account deletion steps are on the deletion page.

1. Who this Policy covers

This Policy applies to:

  • The Keep My People Android app, package com.keepmypeople.
  • The iOS app with the same bundle id, when it is released. The iOS app is in development and is not offered on the App Store at the date of this Policy.
  • This website, which publishes product information, these policies, and a way to email an account-deletion request. The website has no account login.

It does not control Google Play, Google Maps, sign-in screens, or ad screens opened by those companies. Their own policies apply to those surfaces.

2. Information the app collects

Account and profile

When you register we collect an email address and authentication credentials, or the identifiers returned by Google Sign-In if you choose that method. You may add a display name, profile photo, an optional phone number, and a language preference. The interface supports English, Japanese, and Korean.

Groups and invites

We store group names, membership, who invited whom, and invite codes or links, including expiry and how many times an invite may be used. A group is limited to 20 members.

Chat and meetups

We store messages you send in a group, including a location pin or meetup note you choose to post, and the fact that a typing indicator was sent while you were composing. We do not upload your address book.

Location

With your permission, and while location sharing is on, we collect precise location from GPS, Wi-Fi, or the mobile network, together with a timestamp, an accuracy estimate, and related device context such as battery level. If you grant “Allow all the time” and leave sharing on, collection can continue while the app is in the background or after you leave it, so group members can still see you. We keep location history on our servers for about 30 days, then delete it. History is limited to times when sharing was on. Saved places you create (a name, an icon, and coordinates) stay until you delete them or delete the account. The free plan stores 1 saved place. Premium stores up to 10.

Alerts and SOS

We store the alert settings you choose, such as a distance radius, a speed threshold, a battery percent, or an inactivity duration. If you trigger SOS we record that event and, when the phone has one, the last known location, and we notify other members of that group. SOS does not record microphone audio and does not contact public emergency services.

Payments and diamonds

We do not store your full payment card number. Google Play processes charges. We receive purchase tokens, product identifiers, order identifiers, and subscription status so we can unlock Premium, credit diamond packs, and handle refunds or revoked purchases. Diamond balances and which cosmetic frame, icon, hat, or crown you equipped are stored on the account. A rewarded-ad credit is recorded only after Google’s server-side verification says the ad was completed.

Ads on the free plan

Google AdMob may process an advertising identifier and general device information to show ads. Premium removes in-app ads. We do not send your GPS coordinates to the ad network for sale or for foot-traffic products.

Device and diagnostics

We store a push token from Firebase Cloud Messaging so alerts and chat notifications can arrive. If you turn diagnostic data on in Settings, we may receive crash or diagnostic information. That data is not intended to include precise SOS coordinates. Diagnostic data stays off unless you enable it.

Support

If you write through in-app inquiry or email help@trade-arena.net, we keep the message, the reply address, the category you chose, and any screenshot you attach, so we can answer and keep a record of the request.

3. Information this website collects

The pages are static. We do not run an account system here, and we do not set advertising or analytics cookies. If you use the deletion form, it only opens your own email program or shows text for you to copy. The message reaches us when you send it. Your email provider, and ours, then process that message under their own systems. Fonts may be loaded from Google Fonts, which receives the usual technical request your browser makes to download a font file.

4. How we use information

  • Create and protect your account, and show your profile on the map and in chat.
  • Share live location only with members of groups you joined, and only while your sharing switch is on.
  • Deliver place, distance, battery, inactivity, driving, speeding, and SOS alerts according to the settings and plan on the account.
  • Operate invites, chat, meetups, history playback, stay maps, and drive reports.
  • Verify Play purchases, prevent a purchase token from being redeemed on a second account, and reconcile refunds.
  • Show ads to free accounts and credit diamonds when a rewarded ad is verified.
  • Answer support, prevent abuse, and comply with law that applies to a specific request.

5. Who can see it

People in a group with you can see your display name, photo, sharing status, battery level when reported, live location while sharing is on, chat you send to that group, meetup posts, and SOS alerts you send. They cannot see your location from a group you have not joined. When you turn sharing off, new live updates stop.

We use processors that host and operate the Service, including:

  • Supabase, for the database, authentication, file storage, and backend functions.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging, for push notifications.
  • Google Maps, for the map display and for map links posted in chat.
  • Google Sign-In, if you choose it.
  • Google AdMob, for ads shown to free accounts.
  • Google Play, for billing. Apple billing would apply only if an iOS app is later offered.
  • Google Fonts, for typography in the app and on this website.

These providers may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States or the region where they operate. They may use the data to provide their service to us, and their own policies apply where they act independently, such as Maps or ads.

We may disclose information if a binding legal process requires it, or where it is necessary to protect the rights, safety, or security of users, the public, or the Service. We do not sell personal information, and we do not share precise location with third parties for advertising or commercial location-analytics products.

6. Retention

Account data stays until you delete the account. Live location is updated while sharing is on. Location history is kept about 30 days. Chat remains while the group exists, and is removed or anonymized when you leave in the ways described on the deletion page, when the group is deleted, or when the account is deleted. Purchase records are kept as needed to honor subscriptions, refunds, and fraud checks. After the 7-day deletion window we delete or irreversibly anonymize personal data without undue delay, except records that must be kept for a legal, tax, or dispute reason, and only for that period.

7. Your choices

  • Edit your profile, including name and photo.
  • Turn location sharing off, or leave a group.
  • Deny or revoke location, notification, or background permissions in the phone settings. Live map and some safety features then cannot work as designed.
  • Limit ad tracking in the phone’s advertising settings.
  • Turn diagnostic data off.
  • Delete the account in the app under Settings → Delete account, or request deletion from the deletion page. Signing in again within 7 days cancels a pending deletion.
  • Ask for access or correction through in-app support or help@trade-arena.net.

8. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13, or under the higher digital-consent age that applies where the child lives. We do not knowingly collect personal information from a child below that age. A parent or guardian may use their own account. If you believe we have information from a child below that age, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

We use encrypted transport (HTTPS), access controls on the database, and hashed passwords. Purchase checks happen on the server, not by trusting the phone to declare that a plan is active. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your information we will investigate and notify as required by the law that applies to that incident.

10. Advertising

Free-plan ads are served by Google AdMob, including banners and optional rewarded ads in the diamond shop. You can limit personalized ads in device settings. Subscribing to Premium, or uninstalling the app, stops in-app ads. Uninstalling does not delete the account.

11. United States state privacy rights

If you live in California or another U.S. state that gives you rights to know, delete, or correct personal information, or to opt out of “sale” or “sharing” as those statutes define the words, you may use in-app support or help@trade-arena.net. We do not sell personal information as described in this Policy. We will not discriminate against you for exercising those rights. We may need to confirm that the request comes from the account holder.

12. EEA and UK

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we process data to perform the contract (providing the Service you asked for), with your consent (for example precise location, and advertising identifiers where consent is required), and for legitimate interests such as securing the Service and preventing fraud, balanced against your rights. You may withdraw consent, object, or ask for a portable copy by contacting us. You may also complain to your local supervisory authority. Withdrawing location consent means live sharing and the alerts that depend on it will stop.

13. Changes

We may update this Policy. The date at the top will change. If a change is material, we will also give a notice in the app when that is reasonably possible. The copy inside the app may lag this page by a release; if the two differ on a point about the website, this page controls the website, and the in-app copy controls the app until the next release aligns them.
